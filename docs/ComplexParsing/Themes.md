# Field Gameplay Concepts
These represent activities that are done in the field, ala "Open Field Research".

## Sanadforutese
Derived from the term for sand fortress, a kind of shanty town or fortress made out of crashed spaceships.

## Sanadforutese De Liminale
Representing contradictory states within a sand fortress, and more specifically predicting hours of activity.

## Gurasmitene
Literally "Grass Mittens", which roughly translates to having a green thumb, or alternatively sleeping in the grass.

Origins: During the Franco-Japanese wars, dislocated persons might have to sleep in the grass in farmland in extended periods without tents. But came to more generally mean someone experienced with gardening using wild seeds.

# Social
This are for the social simulation ala "Visual Novel" elements of game.

## Gadagecafuse
A cafe made out of a garage to hide research activities. More specifically, this algorithm plots out research hubs in this roguelike that indicate where hidden research activities might be on the map.

# Supernatural And Paranormal Elements
Terms related to specialize divsions of secret police designed to monitor dissident elements in undesireable professions.

## Night Walker Algorithm
This is designed to monitor and analyze patterns in information as if one were trying to blend in with "night walkers" whom take jobs no other types of people will take. In Uploaded Fairy, the connation is jobs like Le Famille Guillotine, other other types of morbid professions. This is the only term made explicitely for the purpose of monitoring a specific family in my alternate history timeline for my science fiction, such as monitoring the gap between their daywalker and night walker activities.

This comes in two variants: Night Walkers and Day Walkers. Day Walkers are adapted to the sunlight, and live similar hours to regular humans.

# Self Defence Gameplay
These terms are related to self-defence aspects of the games.

## Gehadiso 
The Ahuzacos spelling of the Latin term for Gladius, a specific kind of short sword that Roman Legionaires used as backup weapons. Here it is used for testing penetration of analyses of archetypes. In this algorithm, the genetic algorithm specializes in such signaling.
